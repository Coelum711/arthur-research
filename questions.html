01 / Research agenda

Research Questions

One mother question, three core questions and one secondary systems question. These are open questions, not reported findings.

How can a canon-grounded persistent character remain recognisably itself while genuinely changing through open-ended lived experience?

Where should the consequences of an experience stop?

RQ1 / Persistence policy

When should an effect decay as transient state, remain as autobiographical history, or become enduring character change?

Study direction
Compare persistence strategies while keeping identity continuity and meaningful adaptation as separate outcomes.
Evidence requirement
Distinguish remembering an event from changing later behaviour or enduring identity.
See public study directions ↗

Can recognisability survive meaningful change?

RQ2 / Continuity and development

Which canon-grounded commitments should constrain development, and which dispositions can change through lived experience?

Study direction
Evaluate continuity and development independently rather than treating either stability or change as sufficient by itself.
Evidence requirement
Use more than surface style: later decisions, dispositions and recognisability must be assessed separately.
See public study directions ↗

Can one policy reduce both over-consolidation and rebound?

RQ3 / Failure modes

Can a system avoid turning transient effects into character while preserving changes that have adequate support?

Study direction
Test whether a persistence policy can resist both premature hardening and excessive return toward the starting identity.
Evidence requirement
Report the two failure modes separately instead of hiding their trade-off inside one aggregate score.
See public study directions ↗

Secondary question / How do dialogue and out-of-dialogue experience interact?

RQ4 / System-level interaction

Do experiences acquired outside direct dialogue contribute distinct downstream effects, once exposure and retrieval are controlled?

Study direction
Ask whether experience acquired under different interaction regimes leaves distinguishable downstream effects.
Evidence requirement
Separate exposure, memory access and later behaviour; naturalistic trajectories remain hypothesis-generating rather than causal proof.
