06 / Framework changes

Research Log

Versioned changes to the public framing, evidence standards and study plans.

Public changelog only

This page records changes to the public research framework. It intentionally omits researcher identity, private project chronology, internal runtime events and personal records.

Framework v0.1

Persistence depth

The research question was organised around three possible outcomes of experience: transient state, autobiographical retention and enduring character change.

Framework v0.2

Symmetric failure modes

Over-consolidation and rebound / over-reversion were separated so that preserving identity and permitting development could be evaluated independently.

Framework v0.3

Evidence boundaries

Naturalistic observations, controlled causal evidence, literature context and research interpretation were explicitly separated. No private naturalistic trace is included in the public edition.

Framework v0.4

Adjacent literature branches

Belief revision and experience reuse were added because they address how prior experience continues to influence later judgement and action.

For future entries

Record what changed in the public framework, why it changed, what evidence supports the change, unresolved alternatives and the next test. Avoid including personal chronology or private runtime events.
