07 / Position & boundaries

About / Scope

An anonymised public research archive with explicit evidence and privacy boundaries.

Scope

Arthur Research is an anonymised public research archive about canon-grounded persistent characters and selective change through experience.

The site publishes a conceptual framework, proposed methods and related-work notes. It does not identify the researcher or disclose private details about the motivating character system.

Evidence boundaries

Evidence typeWhat it can supportStatus in this edition
Naturalistic observationA documented trajectory in its recorded setting; hypotheses for further tests.No private naturalistic trace published.
Controlled causal evidenceA bounded effect under a defined intervention, comparison and sampling design.Proposed studies only; not run.
LiteratureContext from other work, within each source’s methods and limits.Linked source notes; no replication claimed.
Research interpretationA mechanism, synthesis or design hypothesis to investigate.Working framework; open to revision.

What is outside the present claim

  • No claim that any private production system has demonstrated enduring character change.
  • No causal conclusion from an anecdote, self-report or before/after excerpt alone.
  • No claim that the three persistence levels are already implemented or validated.
  • No claim that recognisability, memory recall and behavioural stability are interchangeable.
  • No claim that the proposed intersection is absent from all prior work.

Privacy boundary

This public edition intentionally contains no researcher real name, educational affiliation, email address, physical location or other real-world biographical information.

It also contains no private character biography, raw activity history, private dialogue, internal journals, relationship details, place history, personal timestamps, runtime credentials, raw system logs, production prompts or production source code.

The project name and public research domain remain visible because they identify the research archive itself. Any future empirical material should be synthetic, public, or specifically redacted for a bounded research claim before publication.

The site uses local styles and ordinary links. It has no analytics, cookies, external fonts, backend or visitor-input collection.

Research development

The public framing treats persistent-character continuity as a stability–plasticity problem: experience should be able to matter without every remembered event becoming a durable trait.

The public archive separates research interpretation from empirical evidence and records framework revisions without exposing private project history.
