The active question sits inside a much larger literature network. This page keeps the newest and most direct neighbours in the foreground while retaining the broader character, memory, autonomy, drift and evaluation landscape behind them.

How to read the map

The constellation is a research-positioning aid, not a quality ranking. Angular sectors are topic clusters. Orbits indicate how directly a work currently bears on the active question: current foreground, closest, adjacent, supporting and background/infrastructure. The eleven axis tags are descriptive coverage dimensions. Multi-domain papers are placed in one primary sector only for readability.

Boundary conditions

Territory already occupied

The point of the atlas is to prevent broad novelty claims that the literature has already absorbed.

Post-canon action by established characters is already covered.

BookWorld and EvolvingWorld already let established fictional characters act beyond source scenes and accumulate new state.

Character growth versus recognisability is already an evaluation problem.

ArcANE, PHASE-Tree, EvolvingWorld, persona-drift work and ANCHOR cover phase-correct state, evolution smoothness, recognisability or long-horizon drift.

Perspective-bounded character memory is already established.

REVERIEMEM, STAGE, BOOKMARKS and DREAM explicitly model epistemic access, first-person memory or story-point validity.

Autonomous long-horizon life and persona evolution are already active areas.

AutoPersonas, Agentopia, OpenLife, PEPA and VibeLifeBench cover compressed or deployed persistent autonomy.

Representation and behaviour can diverge.

Stable Personas, psychometric work and long-term memory probes show that self-report, stored state, retrieval and observable behaviour are not interchangeable.

Memory-induced drift is not a blank space.

DRIFTLENS, MEMDRIFT and SSGM cover reasoning drift, tool drift and semantic memory drift.

Duration alone is not a novelty claim.

OpenLife, Springdrift, Deco and other systems already report real or long-horizon deployments; the research question must depend on the structure of persistence, not simply elapsed time.
